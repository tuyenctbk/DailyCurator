Privacy Policy

DailyCurator is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data in the DailyCurator application.

Data Collection and Usage

DailyCurator does not collect, store, or share any personal user information. All artwork metadata is either cached locally on your device for offline access or fetched from publicly available, open-access museum APIs (The Metropolitan Museum of Art API and the Art Institute of Chicago API).

Permissions

The application requests permission to write to device storage solely to allow users to download artwork images to their device's gallery and to set wallpapers as requested by the user.

Sharing

When you use the share feature, the application passes artwork metadata and a link to the image to your chosen third-party application. We do not track or store the content of what you share.

Contact

If you have any questions, please contact the developer via the email provided in the Play Store listing.
